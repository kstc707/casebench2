# Cofounder lessons

- **Sai thinks best on paper and in his own words.** His notebook pages drove the biggest decisions.
  Ask him to write the anchor, don't write it for him. (Oct 2026)
- **One strong piece of outside feedback can swing the whole direction** (the data chief, "nobody will code
  in the future"). Help him test it against the vision before changing course.
- **He wants control and understanding over speed.** He said fast building made him feel "not in control".
